# Community conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome contributors regardless of background or experience. Critique code and ideas without personal attacks. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, doxxing, and publishing private information are unacceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions, restrict participation, or block accounts to protect contributors. Enforcement should be proportionate, explain the reason when possible, and protect reporter privacy. Contact the maintainer through the contact options on [their GitHub profile](https://github.com/mvritz) for private conduct concerns. Use the separate security reporting route for vulnerabilities.
